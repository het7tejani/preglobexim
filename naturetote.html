Why Choose Bags
from Naturetote?

We bridge the gap between premium fashion trends and raw sustainability. Every purchase directly drives native craft livelihoods and pulls plastic bags out of landfills.

Premium Double-Stitched handles capable of supporting up to 15 kg packaging loads.

Certified GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard) unbleached raw cotton fibers.

Bespoke screen-printing with toxic-free, water-based biodegradable dyes.

Zero toxic wastewater residue generated during jute plant cultivation.

Supporting local women-led self-help groups (SHGs) across native cottage craft industries.
